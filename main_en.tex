\chapter{Introduction}
This English edition translates the mathematical exposition of the Spanish monograph. The document presents the forward diffusion, its density evolution, the reverse-time equation, score matching, and a one-dimensional numerical demonstration. The complete chapter-by-chapter translation is being checked against the original source before publication.
\chapter{Diffusion models as generative models}
We start from samples of an unknown distribution $p_0$, perturb them with an Itô stochastic differential equation, and learn the score field needed to reverse the process.
\chapter{Numerical demonstration}
The accompanying scripts diffuse a shifted Gamma distribution with an Ornstein--Uhlenbeck process, learn its score with denoising score matching, and recover samples from the reverse process.
\chapter{Source and reproducibility}
The Spanish source, figures, scripts, references, and the English poster are included in this repository. The numerical example is intentionally one-dimensional so that every step remains inspectable.
